01 / ABOUT THE SUBJECT

EVEM helped me look at engineering from a different point of view — not only what technology can do, but what responsibility comes with it.

Through this subject, I have explored ideas connected with environmental responsibility, sustainable practices, waste reduction and responsible use of technology.

This portfolio brings together my actual EVEM academic work and my reflections on the importance of building a cleaner and more sustainable future.
